ide-alpha 4

Run ide-alpha-4.exe. Upload your sketch with Auto pin readings enabled.

LiquidCrystal and LiquidCrystal_I2C print calls report their latest printed value to the Dashboard. This reports print calls, not the full LCD screen layout.
The Board view marks unused inputs as unknown. Inputs without pull-up resistors can float. Physical wire attachment cannot be detected reliably by the Uno.
The Parts Library adds parts to the circuit diagram and assigns editable pins. Connect the matching wires on your physical circuit.
